# MINISTRY OF FINANCE — ANNUAL BRIEFING
## The State of Ur Under Amar-Suen, Year 5

*Prepared from the consolidated accounts of Drehem, Umma, and Girsu*
*53 fiscal years on record (Shulgi 46 – Shu-Suen 9)*

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The state's books show a structural livestock deficit. Across 53 years of records, outflows exceed inflows by **268,436 head** — roughly half of all recorded receipts are unmatched by recorded disbursements. This is not a single year's anomaly; it is the persistent condition of the accounts.

Grain accounts are healthier: net positive 4,096 sila across the period. Labor deployment totals 270,642 work-days.

---

### LIVESTOCK ACCOUNT

| | Head |
|---|---|
| Total recorded receipts | 266,962 |
| Total recorded disbursements | 535,398 |
| **Net position** | **−268,436** |

The deficit is concentrated in the Drehem clearinghouse, which disburses roughly two animals for every one received. Provincial contributions (Umma, Girsu) do not close the gap.

**Heaviest outflow years:**
1. Amar-Suen 5 — 52,654 head disbursed
2. Amar-Suen 2 — 45,817 head disbursed
3. Shulgi 46 — 38,614 head disbursed

**Assessment:** Either the state is consuming its herds faster than they reproduce, or — more likely — a significant volume of incoming deliveries was never recorded. The deficit represents a *lower bound* on unrecorded receipts, not a measured loss.

---

### GRAIN ACCOUNT

| | Sila |
|---|---|
| Total recorded receipts | 6,098 |
| Total recorded disbursements | 2,002 |
| **Net position** | **+4,096** |

Grain flows are modest compared to livestock and show a surplus. Umma is the primary grain province. Recording appears more complete here, though volumes are small relative to what the state's population would require — suggesting most grain administration happened outside these three archives.

---

### LABOR ACCOUNT

**270,642 work-days** deployed across the recorded period, primarily in Umma province. This represents corvée and hired labor for agricultural and construction work.

---

### PROVINCIAL CONTRIBUTIONS

- **Drehem** (central clearinghouse): Dominates livestock flows. The imbalance originates here.
- **Umma** (southern province): Primary source of grain and labor records. Modest livestock.
- **Girsu** (southern province): Mixed grain and livestock. 522,000 sheep recorded — a larger pastoral operation than previously assumed.

**Inter-provincial transfers:** 255 officials appear in all three archives. Cross-archive invoice matching is underway to reconcile specific transfers.

---

### RISKS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

1. **The livestock deficit demands explanation.** If real, the state's herds are being depleted. If archival (more likely), we are missing roughly half the incoming delivery records. Either way, the books do not balance.

2. **Grain volumes are implausibly low.** The recorded grain would not feed the recorded labor force. The main grain administration must be elsewhere — possibly at Ur itself, whose archives are lost.

3. **Date coverage is uneven.** Drehem is 98% dated. Umma is 39%. Girsu is 20%. Annual figures for the provinces should be treated as partial.

4. **Recommendation:** Prioritize recovery of Umma and Girsu date coverage to complete the annual series. Pursue transaction-level cross-archive reconciliation to identify which specific transfers are recorded on one side but not the other.

---

*This briefing is derived from 69,138 dated transactions across three archives. Undated records are excluded. All figures are as recorded; no estimates or imputations have been applied.*
